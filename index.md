# Hatchwork — Privacy Policy & Support

*Last updated: November 8, 2026*

Hatchwork is a puzzle game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Hatchwork does not collect any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress (which puzzles you solved, your best times, and unfinished boards) and your settings are stored on your device.

## iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Hatchwork keeps a copy of your puzzle progress in your own iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage. The developer cannot see or access this data. You can turn off iCloud for Hatchwork in the Settings app on your device.

## Photos

When you share a solved picture from the Gallery, the system share sheet may offer "Save Image". If you choose it, iOS asks your permission to **add** that one picture to your photo library. Hatchwork can only add pictures you choose to save; it never reads, browses or uploads your photos.

## Purchases

The one-time "Full Version" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Hatchwork only receives a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid. The developer never sees your payment details or your Apple ID.

## Children

Hatchwork does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
